Frequently asked questions.

Short answers about how we work. For questions about your own business, book a consultation.

What does Zetavai do?

+

Zetavai is a technology consulting and implementation partner. We combine strategy, consulting and implementation in one team, and design and build connected digital tools that save time and help businesses grow.

Who do you work with?

+

We work with SMEs, MSMEs and manufacturers, and with professionals and small business owners such as consultants, trainers, planners and creative studios.

What do you mean by a digital ecosystem?

+

A set of connected tools that work together: a digital presence that brings customers in, business tools that handle the daily work, and data that shows what is happening. Each part is useful on its own; connected, they run part of your business for you.

Do we need to replace the software we already use?

+

No. We connect new tools to the systems you already rely on, such as your ERP, accounting software, spreadsheets, calendar and email, and only recommend replacing something when it clearly pays off.

How does a project start?

+

With a complimentary 30-minute consultation. If there is a good fit, we either run a short diagnostic that ends in a roadmap, or agree a defined project. Scope, deliverables, timeline and price are agreed in writing before work begins.

How much does a project cost?

+

It depends on scope. After the consultation we send a written proposal with a fixed scope and price, so you know the cost before you commit.

Do you use AI in your solutions?

+

Where it genuinely helps. Typical uses are extracting and structuring information from documents and other unstructured data, and analysing your own business data. We recommend AI only when it is the simplest reliable option.

Where are you based?

+

We are based in Pune, India, and work with clients remotely. Our team brings delivery experience from India and Germany.
