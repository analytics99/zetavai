Questions from professionals.

What is the difference between a static and a dynamic website?

+

A static website presents your business: who you are, what you offer and how to reach you. A dynamic website adds tools behind it, such as enquiry forms that are stored and managed, booking calendars, quote generators or personalised client plans.

Can clients book appointments directly on my website?

+

Yes. We build booking and calendar tools into your website, connected to your own calendar.

Can my website generate quotes or plans for clients?

+

Yes. We build dynamic quote generators and personalised client plans, for example training plans for a fitness trainer.

Will my website work well on mobile?

+

Yes. Every website and tool we build is mobile-ready, because most of your clients will visit on their phone.
